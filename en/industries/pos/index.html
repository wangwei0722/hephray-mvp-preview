Retail floor

POS

A store computer is furniture until it stops. HEPHRAY treats POS hosts as industrial platforms: power that can be yanked, ports that must match the cable already in the cabinet, and a service model that does not start with unscrewing a bezel in front of a queue.

Stay on through the trading day

POS duty is long, dusty, and electrically rude. Automatic power-on, reverse-polarity tolerance, and a dense I/O map keep the host aligned with printers, scanners, cash drawers, and customer displays that were specified years ago.

Service without theatre

BIOS and Management Engine updates can move without opening the chassis. Forced-shutdown recovery and health monitoring mean a brutal close-of-day power cut is a recoverable event, not a truck roll.
