Cybersecurity

Network-security hardware is often a locked box with a strict story about who may open it. The platform still has to recover a failed switch path, a dead USB lane, and a display port that should not speak out of turn.

Buttons and ports as policy

A power button can encode reset, service, or display hand-off. Display-port governance keeps output on a policy, not on whoever plugged in a monitor. That is how a sealed chassis stays sealed.
