T1

In-chassis BIOS / ME update

Pain

A sealed POS or lottery host still needs BIOS / ME refresh — opening the chassis is not an option.

Approach

Field platforms stay sealed. Service windows shrink because firmware can move over a governed channel instead of a screwdriver.

Forced-shutdown recovery

Pain

Unattended terminals get yanked from the wall; the next boot must come back on its own.

Approach

Unattended terminals are pulled from the wall. The next boot should diagnose, restore, and report — not wait for a bench visit.
