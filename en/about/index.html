Name

HEPHRAY / 顚锐

The English name is HEPHRAY, pronounced /HEF-ray/. The Chinese name is 顚锐. The line we work under is Foresee. Forge. Forth. — see the fault before it becomes a queue, forge the platform that can take it, then put the site back into motion.

Stance

An independent brand site

This site speaks only for HEPHRAY. It is a solutions and field narrative — not a product SKU list, and not a corporate family tree. Public pages, metadata, and the mailbox use the hephray.com name alone.

Craft

Industrial computing for sealed, unattended rooms

The work is firmware you can reach without opening a chassis, recovery after a brutal power cut, I/O maps that respect the cable already in the cabinet, and governance of buttons and display ports on appliances that must stay locked. We ship platforms into ten countries, more than one hundred customers, and over five million units.

Mark

Pale field, graphite type, one accent

The public site is a pale B2B field: paper #FFFFFF, mist #F4F8F9, graphite type #42424C / #29292E, and one accent #FF861C. Wordmark and slogan stay HEPHRAY / 顚锐 — Foresee. Forge. Forth. The mark is a crystal cut on a light page, not a dark industrial copper board.
